Bokeh interactive DDS explorer for Lecanemab.

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  SIGNIFICANCE
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Linked brushing: select formulations in one panel, linked panels update. Ideal for large datasets (100+ formulations).

  SCIENTIFIC BASIS
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Bokeh WebGL rendering. ColumnDataSource enables O(1) updates.

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Select formulations with box/lasso select. Click 'Reset' to clear selection.
